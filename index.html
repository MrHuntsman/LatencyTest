⚡ System Latency Test

Measure click-to-photon latency with no extra hardware — your PC flashes on every click, your phone hears and sees it, and the gap is your latency.

🖥️

PC — Emitter

Run this on the computer being tested. It goes fullscreen and flashes white on every mouse click or key press, and logs the browser's own click→paint delay.

Open emitter → 📱

Phone — Sensor

Run this on your phone. Point the camera at the screen, place the phone near the mouse, and it measures the time between hearing the click and seeing the flash.

Open sensor →

How it works

  1. Open the Emitter on your PC, go fullscreen, keep the tab in the foreground.
  2. Open the Sensor on your phone (same Wi-Fi is not required — nothing is transmitted).
  3. Fill the target box on the phone with your monitor. Tap Start, then click the mouse ~1× per second.
  4. The phone pairs each audible click with the flash that follows and reports the median latency.

Everything runs locally in your browser. No video or audio is recorded or uploaded.
